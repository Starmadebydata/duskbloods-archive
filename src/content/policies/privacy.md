This policy is for **duskbloodsarchive.com**, published by **Wind Flash Studio**. It was last updated on 24 August 2026. Questions go to **contact@duskbloodsarchive.com**.

## What this site is

Duskbloods Archive is an unofficial English guide. It has no user accounts, no comments, and no forms that ask for your name, except the act of sending us email.

## Information we receive

**Email you send us.** If you write to contact@duskbloodsarchive.com, we receive whatever you include in that message, plus standard mail metadata (your address, date, and related headers). We use it to reply, correct pages, or handle copyright requests. We do not use that inbox to build advertising audiences.

**Hosting and security logs.** The site is served through Cloudflare. Like most public sites, requests can create technical logs: IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser type, date and time, referring URL, and the pages requested. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the site, absorb abuse, and keep TLS working. We do not sell these logs.

**No Nintendo credentials.** Do not send Nintendo Account passwords, download codes, or payment details. We will not ask for them.

## Cookies, beacons, and identifiers

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. A web beacon or pixel is a tiny request that helps a service know a page was viewed. An identifier can include a cookie ID, a mobile advertising ID, or an IP address.

This site may use:

- cookies or similar storage needed to load the page or remember a basic setting;
- Cloudflare tools that measure anonymous page views;
- Google advertising and measurement cookies **if and when** Google AdSense or related Google ads run on these pages.

We do not currently place our own login cookies, because there is no login.

## Google advertising (including AdSense)

Google, as a third-party vendor, may use cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also visit [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/) to opt out of some third-party personalized advertising.

Third parties, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, and IP addresses to collect information, measure ads, and personalize ads. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If ads are not yet visible on a given page, this section still describes what Google advertising technologies would do when they are enabled. We will not run ads on screens that have no publisher content.

## Children

This archive is written for a general game-information audience, not as a service directed at children. The game itself may be unsuitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not send us information about a child.

## Europe, the UK, and similar consent rules

If we serve Google personalized ads to visitors in regions that require prior consent, we will present a consent choice before those ads run. Until that mechanism is in place, we will not treat those visits as a place to build sensitive advertising audiences.

## How long we keep information

Email is kept long enough to handle the request and any required follow-up. Security logs follow Cloudflare's retention for the products in use. We do not keep a marketing profile of readers, because we do not run reader accounts.

## Your requests

To ask what we hold from your email, or to ask us to delete a message you sent, write to **contact@duskbloodsarchive.com**. We may need you to confirm you control that address.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a material way—especially if we add accounts, comments, or new advertising partners—we will update this page and the date above.
