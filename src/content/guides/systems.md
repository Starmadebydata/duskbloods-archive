## Virtue

Only the Bloodsworn with the highest Virtue in the second and third phases survive the culling. Ways to earn it in the test include:

- Repulsion Virtue from great foes and trespassers.
- Flame Virtue from lighting skull lanterns; larger lantern corpses pay more, but only to the first lighter.
- Sword Virtue from defeating Bloodsworn at ritual sites.
- Pledge Virtue from swearing at blood pledge pillars. Another player can overwrite your pledge.
- Commendation Virtue at phase end for the top two scores in Bloodfang (defeats) and Collector (exploration items).

Redstones increase Sword Virtue. Goldstones increase Pledge Virtue. You can hold only one stone type at a time.

The final phase grants extra HP based on Virtue earned so far, and the grail gives each of the three remaining Bloodsworn two lives.

## Alliances

Petition a Bloodsworn you encounter. If they accept, you become confederates until the final phase. Confederates cannot harm each other and share Sword Virtue and Pledge Virtue earned at ritual sites. Alliances dissolve before Moon Blood Bestowal, except under Bloodbound Alliances rules, where pairs stay together through the end.

## Sigils

A Sigil etches a relationship and a unique objective that can award Virtue. The Network Test has four:

- Sigil of a Sworn Enemy
- Sigil of an Old Friend
- Sigil of the Eye of Muralis
- Sigil of Love Ephemeral

The guide says these can be changed freely in the release version. Some emotes matter for Sigil goals.
