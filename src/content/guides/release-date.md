The first sentence is the whole point. Official materials still say 2026, and nothing more precise.

## Confirmed

- FromSoftware's April 2, 2025 press release scheduled the game for 2026 on Nintendo Switch 2.
- The US Nintendo product page lists the release date as 2026.
- The same store page did not show a preorder date in the information checked for this article.

## Rumor correction: September 24

Eurogamer asked Nintendo about a 24 September date that appeared at the end of The Guardian's preview coverage of the game. A Nintendo spokesperson answered that the date was inaccurate because there is no information regarding the release date of The Duskbloods. Eurogamer's report also noted that other publications on the same trip described the game only as coming later this year, and that the date surfaced one day before the closed network test began.

That denial is the current official position. This site will not recycle the date as if it were waiting for confirmation.

## What this page will not do

- Treat a shop placeholder, a leaked screenshot, or a countdown as a launch day.
- Turn "later this year" phrasing from preview trips into a calendar entry.
- Report leaked footage as proof of timing. The same report said leaked gameplay appeared online and was later removed.

## Still unknown

Month, day, and price. Whether a preorder will open before a final date is named. Whether any extra network test arrives before launch.
