The first sentence is the whole point: **official materials still say 2026, and nothing more precise.**

## Confirmed

- FromSoftware's April 2, 2025 press release scheduled the game for 2026 on Nintendo Switch 2.
- The US Nintendo product page still lists the release date as 2026.
- The same store page does not offer a preorder date in the information checked for this article.

## Rumor correction: September 24

Eurogamer asked Nintendo about a 24 September date that appeared at the end of The Guardian's preview. A Nintendo spokesperson said the date was inaccurate because there is no information regarding the release date of The Duskbloods.

That denial is the current official position. This site will not recycle the date as if it were pending confirmation.

## What this page will not do

- It will not treat a shop placeholder, a leaked screenshot, or a countdown as a launch day.
- It will not convert "later this year" color from preview trips into a calendar entry.

## Still unknown

- Month, day, and price.
- Whether a preorder will open before a final date is named.
- Whether any extra network test will arrive before launch.
