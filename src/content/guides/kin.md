Those who enter an agreement with the Order of Dusk are known as Kin. They compact with Bloodsworn and fight beside them.

## How summoning works

Compacts form at summoning circles found at the base of chains of light. There are three circle types in the test:

- Ring
- Blade
- Eye

Each circle offers a different kind of Kin. The House of Night courtyard can also let you compact with any Kin for practice.

## What the guide confirms about the eight

The Network Test contains eight Kin. The official overview describes examples rather than a full labeled roster: a young swordswoman of a clandestine society, a sorcerer who has mastered flame, a prisoner who can protect allies by singing, and a black wolf who lords over a forest. This archive will not fill the remaining names from datamines or guesses.

## Growth and death

Kin gain experience when you defeat enemies with them, raising health and attack, and some gain new attacks by level. Smaller summoning circles can appear as a regional feature and grant a large amount of Kin experience.

If a Kin dies, it is dismissed and cannot return for a time. While dismissed or out of combat, its HP recovers gradually. You cannot dismiss a Kin while it is being attacked.
