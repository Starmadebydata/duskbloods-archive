The network test was a pre-release build used to stress servers, watch multiplayer behavior, and collect balance data. It was not the finished game.

## Confirmed facts

- US applications opened July 22, 2026 at 7:00 a.m. PDT and closed July 28 at 6:59 a.m. PDT.
- Selected players were announced August 7 at 6:00 a.m. PDT, in sign-up order.
- Game data and download codes were scheduled for August 18 at 6:00 a.m. PDT.
- A Nintendo Switch 2 and an active Nintendo Switch Online membership were required.
- Matches were limited to eight players.
- Official US session times:
  - Aug 21, 3:00 a.m.–7:00 a.m. PDT
  - Aug 21, 7:00 p.m.–11:00 p.m. PDT
  - Aug 22, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. PDT
  - Aug 23, 3:00 a.m.–7:00 a.m. PDT
  - Aug 23, 7:00 p.m.–11:00 p.m. PDT
- The official page warned that session times could change or be canceled without notice.

Nintendo's US product page described the Global Network Test as taking place from August 21–24. The test site itself listed five sessions ending late on August 23. This archive treats the five PDT sessions as the operational schedule.

## What happened in the first session

Eurogamer reported that testers were stuck on the title screen and unable to enter matches. FromSoftware then closed the first session early, apologized for game-server issues, and said it would announce future schedules later. That is reporting of an official statement, not a new Nintendo support article.

If you still need the login-error walkthrough, use the [errors page](/network-test/errors/).

## What players can do now

- Do not expect a leftover public download code. Selection already happened.
- Game issues belong on official Nintendo or FromSoftware channels, not on this unofficial archive.
- Treat test-build rules as [test-build only](/gameplay/) until the release version repeats them.
- Watch official channels for any extra test, not store-placeholder dates.

## Still unknown

- Whether a makeup session will be offered to people locked out of the first window.
- Whether progress, unlocks, or archive data from the test carry into the retail game. No official carry-over promise was published on the pages checked for this recap.
- The next public playable date.
