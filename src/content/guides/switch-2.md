## Confirmed

- Platform: Nintendo Switch 2 only, in every official product description checked for this page.
- Developer and publisher on the US store: FromSoftware / FromSoftware, Inc., with Nintendo also listed on the debut release as publisher alongside FromSoftware.
- Online features require Nintendo Switch Online (sold separately) and a Nintendo Account. Membership auto-renews unless canceled.
- Store play modes: TV, tabletop, handheld.
- Players: single system (1); online (1–8).
- Language listed on the US page: American English.
- Release window: 2026.

## About solo play

The store's "single system (1)" line confirms the software can be used by one person on one system. FromSoftware still describes the product as a PvPvE title with online multiplayer at its core. The Network Test gameplay guide is written around eight-player Dusk Battles, with a House of Night for practice.

That is not enough to claim a complete offline campaign, a story mode, or a promised solo ladder. Those remain unknown.

## Still unknown

- Any later platform.
- How much of the game functions with NSO expired.
- A full single-player mode beyond local practice tools.
