Official pages agree on the platform. FromSoftware's debut press release calls The Duskbloods a PvPvE title developed for Nintendo Switch 2, with online multiplayer at its core and up to eight players. The US Nintendo store page sells only a Nintendo Switch 2 edition and lists FromSoftware as developer and publisher. No other platform appears on either page.

## Nintendo Switch Online and accounts

Online features require a Nintendo Switch Online membership and a Nintendo Account. The store notes the membership is sold separately and auto-renews at the then-current price unless you cancel. The network test applied the same rule: applicants needed a Switch 2 system and an active membership.

## What the store sheet confirms

- Play modes: TV mode, tabletop mode, and handheld mode.
- Players: single system (1) and online (1 to 8).
- Language: American English.
- Rating: the ESRB box says the game may contain content inappropriate for children and points readers to esrb.org for rating information.
- The product description says Bloodsworn are supernatural beings imbued with special abilities by the power of blood, fighting in online battles of up to eight players for First Blood.

## About solo play

The store's "single system (1)" line confirms the software can be used by one person on one system. The gameplay guide is written around eight-player Dusk Battles, with a House of Night for learning controls and practicing in the courtyard. That is not enough to promise an offline campaign, a story mode, or a full solo ladder. Until Nintendo or FromSoftware says more, treat solo play as unknown, not denied.

## Still unknown

Any later platform, how much of the game works with an expired membership, and a full single-player mode beyond local practice tools.
